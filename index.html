5 Ways to Make Your Phone Battery Last Longer

1. Reduce screen brightness. A bright screen can use significant battery power.

2. Use Battery Saver. Turn it on when you need your battery to last longer.

3. Check battery usage. Your phone settings can show which apps use the most power.

4. Manage background activity. Restrict unnecessary activity for apps you rarely use.

5. Keep your phone updated. Install updates from your phone's official settings.

How to Fix a Slow Android Phone

Restart your phone, check how much storage remains and update your apps.

Remove apps you no longer need. You can also clear an app's cache from Settings when appropriate.

Avoid deleting unfamiliar system files or installing untrusted phone-cleaning apps.

How to Avoid Phone and Online Scams

Never share your banking PIN, password or verification code with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

Check website addresses carefully before signing in. Avoid suspicious links and unexpected downloads.

When in doubt, contact the company through its official website or phone number.
