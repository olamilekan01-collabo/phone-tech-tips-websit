How to Free Up Storage Space on an Android Phone

Running out of storage space? Here are simple ways to create more room on your Android phone.

1. Delete Unwanted Apps

Check your installed apps and uninstall the ones you no longer use.

2. Remove Unnecessary Downloads

Open your Downloads folder and delete files you no longer need.

3. Clear App Cache

Go to Settings, open Apps, select an app, choose Storage and tap Clear cache if available. Avoid Clear data unless you understand that it may remove saved app information.

4. Back Up Photos and Videos

Back up important pictures and videos to a trusted storage service or computer before deleting local copies.

5. Delete Duplicate Files

Look for duplicate pictures, videos and documents that you no longer need.

6. Check Your Storage Settings

Open Settings and look for Storage. Your phone may show which files and apps are using the most space.

Final Tip

Always check your files before deleting them. Keep a backup of anything important.
