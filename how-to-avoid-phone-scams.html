How to Avoid Phone Scams and Fake Links

Learn simple ways to protect your Android phone and personal information from online scams.

1. Avoid suspicious links

Don't tap links from unknown people or unexpected messages. Some links lead to fake websites.

2. Never share your passwords

Keep your passwords and verification codes private. Genuine support workers should not ask you to reveal them.

3. Check websites carefully

Before entering personal information, check the website address. Be careful of websites pretending to be banks or popular companies.

4. Be careful with prizes and giveaways

Messages claiming you have won a prize you never entered for may be scams. Don't pay money to claim suspicious prizes.

5. Download apps from trusted sources

Use trusted app stores and check an app's developer and reviews before installing it.

6. Keep your phone updated

Install security updates when available to help protect your device.
