5 Ways to Make Your Phone Battery Last Longer

Does your phone battery run down quickly? Here are five simple ways to help your battery last longer.

1. Reduce screen brightness

Your screen uses a lot of battery power. Lower your brightness or enable automatic brightness.

2. Use battery saver mode

Turn on Battery Saver when your battery is running low. It helps reduce background activity.

3. Check battery usage

Open your phone settings and check which apps are using the most battery power.

4. Manage background activity

Restrict unnecessary background activity for apps that you rarely use.

5. Keep your phone updated

Install updates from your phone's official settings to receive improvements and fixes.

Final advice

Small changes to your phone settings can help you get more time from each battery charge.
